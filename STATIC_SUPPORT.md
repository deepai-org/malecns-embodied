# Missing mechanical support, not just weak neural output

The latest diagnostic changes the next priority: complete coherent mechanics
across the unpowered leg coordinates before using further collapsing-body trials
to judge neural competence. Increasing torque on only the existing eighteen
coordinates is not a demonstrated solution.

## Question and result

`probe_static_support.py` takes the four residents' recorded poses at one and
two seconds from `passive-original-driven-001`. These are previously upright
poses, not generated by a new optimization. It freezes velocity and asks whether
ground contact forces and selected joint torques can balance gravity **without
the original joint springs**. It never sends its solutions to the simulator.

Latest receipt: `evidence/static-support-003.json`.

| Available leg actuation | Results across eight pose/resident cases |
| --- | --- |
| None | Seven reported infeasible; one numerically inconclusive |
| Current 18 coordinates, original torque limits | Eight reported infeasible |
| Current 18 coordinates, unlimited torque | Six reported infeasible; two numerically inconclusive |
| All 42 leg coordinates, original torque limits | Eight feasible force-balance solutions |

A second optimization minimizes the sum of absolute torques on the 24 currently
unpowered coordinates, while permitting unlimited torque on the existing 18.
It finds positive minimum missing-reaction totals of 1.087–1.883 model torque
units across all eight cases. Maximum balance residual is 5.3e-8 model force/torque
units. These are mathematical solutions under the assumptions below, not measured
muscle strengths. Individual torque allocations need not be unique.

Missing reactions appear at coxa yaw/pitch and tibia–tarsus coordinates, among
others. This does not mean every missing coordinate needs an independent motor:
anatomical muscle coupling, joint constraints and passive tissues can transmit
these forces. It does mean the current unsupported-coordinate approximation is
a serious confound. A global muscle-gain increase cannot be assumed to repair it.

The all-42 solutions have largest individual torques of 1.63–7.87, below the
original ±30 actuator capacities. This objective minimizes contact-ray weight,
not motor torque, so those values are **not** minimum muscle-strength requirements.

## Exact scope and limitations

- Equilibrium equations cover the resident root's six degrees of freedom and
  42 leg actuator coordinates. Other joints are assumed ideally locked. This is
  a favorable mechanical relaxation, not our actual free whole-body dynamics.
- Contact points are reconstructed from the pinned original scene at recorded
  poses. Ground/ecology contacts include positive contact margins; online ecology
  changes are not reproduced. No self-contact or contact with other flies is used.
- Friction uses nonnegative pyramidal rays with the contact's sliding, torsional
  and rolling coefficients as applicable. The contact Jacobian uses geom2 minus
  geom1, following [MuJoCo's convention](https://mujoco.readthedocs.io/en/latest/computation/).
- Joint passive forces, dynamic inertia, aerodynamics, muscle recruitment and
  neural activity are excluded. No assertion about other postures or dynamic
  walking follows from pose-specific static infeasibility.
- HiGHS numerical status 4 is explicitly inconclusive, not an infeasibility proof.
  An earlier commentary miscounted the unbounded case as seven infeasible/one
  inconclusive; both saved runs 002 and 003 actually show six/two.

The box-on-plane unit test checks that contact rays support known gravitational
load and that reversing their sign makes this impossible. It passed on Vast with
MuJoCo 3.12.0. The current runner and all input hashes are recorded in run 003.
Runs 001 and 002 retain the preceding bounded/unbounded variants; their runner
hashes intentionally differ. They are exploratory receipts, not independent
biological replicates or a claim of formal infeasibility certificates.

## Why measured stiffness was not installed

The [passive-force study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12324252/)
supports nonzero passive mechanics and reports collapse after motor silencing.
However, the accessible version labels stiffness inconsistently in its table,
discussion and model methods. Its modeled joint grouping also differs from this
rig. We did not resolve those units, so no purportedly measured stiffness was
copied into the model. The existing zero-passive scene remains a diagnostic
ablation, not our intended final biological body.

## Reproduce

```sh
.venv/bin/python probe_static_support.py \
  --scene ../chreatures/native/fly-body/scenes/training-4/world.json \
  --trial evidence/trials/passive-original-driven-001 \
  --muscle-receipt evidence/trials/coxa-driven-001/result.json \
  --output runs/static-support.json
.venv/bin/python -m unittest -v test_static_support
```

The output path must be fresh. No neural model, scene, controller or background
service is modified. The goal remains rich whole-fly behavior; none is claimed.
